How to Use

  1. Pick a gallery poster or upload your own.
  2. Review existing AOIs or choose a label and draw regions; click a region to remove it.
  3. Click Start and allow webcam access. The page goes full screen and a camera guide shows how far to sit (45–70 cm, about arm's length) and whether you're centred; calibration begins once every check is green.
  4. Look at the small centre of each orange dot until its ring closes — no clicking. Then do the same for the blue accuracy dots.
  5. Look at the poster — recording begins automatically. Every 30 s a dot appears briefly; look at it.
  6. Click Stop, then ↓ CSV for analysis (and ↓ Report to keep the accuracy numbers).
In the main Poster PES app, open Analyze gaze file, upload this CSV and the same poster, then review attention metrics and heatmaps.
